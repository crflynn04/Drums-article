My Experience Playing the Drums

How I started

Playing the drums is a test in multitasking and a test of the relationship with anyone you are living with. I started playing the drums about four years ago at my parents’ house.

Some of the fundamentals

If you want to learn the drums, there are a few things you need to learn first. The first thing is how to hold the sticks. It is not fun taking lessons a year into playing only to learn that you have been doing it incorrectly.

What it's like to play them

Drums are powerful, and you can feel them through your entire body when you play. No matter how softly you play, they are going to be loud. Most drummers I know love that loud energy.

The difficult parts of the drums

Like I said before, drums are loud. If you play them, you basically have to accept that people around you may dislike the noise. It can make it difficult for other people to hear themselves or enjoy a quiet environment.

Thanks for reading about my experience playing the drums!
