My experience playing the Drums

How I started

Playing the drums is a test in multitasking and a test of the relationship with anyone you are living with. I started playing the drums about 4 years ago at this point in my parents' basement.

Some of the fundamentals

If you want to learn the drums, there are a few things you need to learn first. The first thing is how to hold the sticks. Believe me, it is not fun taking lessons a year into playing only to find out you have been holding them incorrectly.

What it's like to play them

Drums are powerful, and you can feel them through your entire body when you play. No matter how softly you play them, they are going to be loud. Most drummers I know love that loud energy.
